Yes — absolutely. We operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year including all major holidays. When you call, a live dispatcher picks up immediately — there's no after-hours answering service or callback queue. A licensed plumber is always on standby and ready to roll.

Our response time guarantee is 60 minutes or less in our primary service area (Cresco & Monroe County). In most cases, we arrive even faster. We station trucks throughout the region so there's always a technician close by.
